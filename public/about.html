Notice

A list of things to do around Jan Juc and the Surf Coast, kept by people who live here.

What it is

The Notice Board is one list. Places you can go any time — beaches, walks, skateparks, cafés, playgrounds — and things that are on, with a date. You can narrow it by what you feel like doing, what kind of thing it is, which town it is in, how far you are willing to drive, and what the weather is doing.

There is no account and nothing to sign up for. Pinning something keeps it in your own browser and nowhere else, which also means it does not follow you to your phone.

Where the listings come from

Some were researched by hand. Some arrive twice a week from the Surf Coast Shire's events calendar, and some are read straight off a venue's own gig listing or ticket page. Anything that arrives automatically waits to be checked by a person before it is marked as verified.

What it will not do

It will not guess. A date with nothing behind it is left blank rather than estimated, a link is never invented, and a listing with no fixed location carries no map pin instead of an approximate one. This is deliberate: a wrong pin is harder to spot than a missing one, and a date that turns out to be from last year sends someone to a locked hall.

Distances are approximate driving distances from Jan Juc, not straight lines — the Great Ocean Road makes those differ by a long way. Some are still unchecked.

Adding something

There is an Add button on the Notice Board. Anything added that way is held as unverified until somebody has looked at it. Local knowledge is the point — the thing you would actually tell a friend, not tourist copy.

Getting it wrong

If a date has moved, a place has closed, or something here is simply incorrect, that is worth knowing. Every correction so far has come from somebody noticing one row disagreeing with another.
