Runs

Events

Things that happen on a date and then are over. A date is what separates these from everything else — so what matters here is when, where, and how well the date is actually known. Recurring ones are still events; the How often filter holds the list down to the genuine one-offs.

Activities

The evergreen half — places and things that are simply there, with no date on them. What matters here is whether each one can be found: a real coordinate, a driving distance, and a link that points at a place rather than a search for one.

Places

The venues both of the above hang off — a curated, geocoded row per building. Linking an event to one of these is what gives it a pin, a suburb and a line on the map. Filling in a place’s events_url is also the entire registry for the venue scraper: it reads any row that has one.
