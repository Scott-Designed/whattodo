What this mock-up needs that does not exist

Five new types. tide, wildflower, wildlife, surf, sky — plus waterfall, fungi, paragliding and fire on the rows below the fold. Adding one is five places, and this asks for nine.

Three rows have no icon — whale season, shearwaters and the fire danger period. That is the real behaviour, not a gap in the mock: the slot is always drawn so every name stays on the same left edge, and Lucide has no whale and no flame. Look at the left gutter on those three.

A row that is off is dimmed, never hidden. The five at the bottom are real listings waiting on weather — no rain, no southerly, Kp only 3. A reader wants to know the waterfall is low, not to find it missing.

The When column is doing something new. “Needs a southerly” and “Needs Kp 6+” are not dates. Today the column holds a date or a recurrence, and half of these rows have neither — they have a condition. That is the schema question this whole exploration keeps arriving at.

The ON NOW badge already works for this. It is on three rows here and it needed no new mechanism — it reads a start and an end. A season has both, so the badge that says a gig is playing can say the orchids are out.

Everything else is the board as built. The tint down the left is the group colour, the pin saves, the open row carries the description and the detail list. Nothing here is a new component.
